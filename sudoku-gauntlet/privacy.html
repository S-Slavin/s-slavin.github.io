Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Sudoku Gauntlet ("the App"). This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

Data stored on your device

The App stores game progress locally on your device, including:

  • Puzzle progress, stats, and best times
  • Daily streak and achievement progress
  • Daily gauntlet progress and ghost run times for self-comparison
  • Settings (sound, haptics, theme, notifications)
  • Optional nickname and anonymous player ID for leaderboards

This data stays on your device unless you choose to sync a score to our server.

Leaderboard (optional)

If you complete a daily puzzle while online, the App may send your nickname, anonymous player ID, puzzle date, difficulty, completion time, and hints used to our API to display rankings. No email or account is required.

Advertising

The free version of the App may show ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads. You can remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase. On iOS, you may be asked for permission to track activity across apps for personalized ads.

Notifications

If you enable Daily Reminder in Settings, the App schedules a local notification on your device. No push notification server stores your data.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
